# Engineering & Data Decisions Log (`DECISIONS.md`)

| ID | Stage / Lane | Decision | Alternatives Considered | Why & Verified Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **DEC-001** | Stage 1 (Data) | Populate `city = 'Atlanta'` in `a4i_core.heat_readings` via `COALESCE(NULLIF(TRIM(CAST(city AS STRING)), ''), 'Atlanta')` | Leave `city` NULL or modify `a4i_climate.heat_observations` in place | Raw `a4i_climate.heat_observations` has `100% NULL` (`23,386/23,386`) in `city`, causing `validate.sql` check `heat: city label present` to fail. Ground rules forbid modifying raw `a4i_climate`; populating in `a4i_core.heat_readings` preserves raw immutability while fixing downstream city filters. |
| **DEC-002** | Stage 1 (Data) | Strip Census `-666666666` and `0` sentinels to `NULL` in `a4i_core.tracts` (`median_age <= 0`, `median_income <= 0`, `median_year_structure_built NOT BETWEEN 1900 AND 2025`) | Drop tracts with sentinels, or impute with city mean in `a4i_core` | Raw `tract_demographics` contains `-666666666` in `median_age` (`3` unpopulated tracts), `median_income` (`4` tracts, **`2` of which have `total_pop > 0`**), and `median_year_structure_built` (`7` rows `-666666666` + `4` rows `0`). Leaving `-666666666` corrupts population-weighted city median income to `-$631,111.79`; cleaning to `NULL` restores the true population-weighted city baseline (`$75,714.39` across `350` populated tracts) without dropping tracts. |
| **DEC-003** | Stage 1 & 2 (Data) | Flag stationary vehicle clusters (`neighbors_within_20m >= 10`, `526` rows / `2.25%`) and `±4 SD` temperature outliers (`0` rows) as boolean columns in `a4i_core.heat_readings`; aggregate to `geohash-7` (`~153 m`) cells in `a4i_semantic.fact_heat_cell` (`WHERE NOT is_temp_outlier`) | Hard-delete stationary points or 4-decimal near-duplicates (`4,052` excess rows) in `a4i_core` | Preserving all `23,386` range-valid readings in `a4i_core.heat_readings` with boolean flags (`is_stationary_cluster`, `is_temp_outlier`) makes cleaning auditable and reversible, while aggregating to `2,020` `geohash-7` cells in `fact_heat_cell` collapses `1 Hz` stationary dwell points and road autocorrelation to one weight per ~150 m cell. |
| **DEC-004** | Stage 1 (Data) | Join `censustract_2020_5yr` (sum of 12 male/female `65+` age columns) and `us_census_tracts_national` (`area_land_meters`, `area_water_meters`) on `LPAD(geo_id, 11, '0')` | Use ACS 2018 5-yr or approximate tract area from bounding box | `censustract_2020_5yr` is the newest ACS table in `bigquery-public-data.census_bureau_acs` and achieves a `100%` (`353/353`) join match with `0` population discrepancies (`pop_65_plus = 180,569`, `12.47%` of `1,447,970` total population) and `100%` (`353/353`) land/water area match (`0.091–42.008 km²`). |
| **DEC-005** | Stage 2 (Data) | Define daily city heat (`fact_city_heat_day`) using `MAX(max_temp_f)` across the `8` Atlanta GHCN stations (`11.1–39.4 km` from traverse centroid) and restrict annual metrics (`metric_city_heat_days_by_year`, `site_hot_days`) to full years (`>= 300` days reported) | Average across stations on each date or include partial years | `MAX(max_temp_f)` across reporting stations (`2,557` days across `2019–2025`, all `7` years having `365–366` days) answers whether official Atlanta weather records reached a heat threshold on that date (`23.9` days/yr `>= 95 °F`, `56.0` days/yr `>= 95 °F`-equivalent at a `+3.5 °F` offset site). |
